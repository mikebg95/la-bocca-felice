About

The happy mouth

La Bocca Felice means “the happy mouth”, and that is really all we are after. Good pasta, great pizza and a glass of wine, in a place where you feel at home.

We are a neighbourhood Italian in Amsterdam for every kind of evening: a quick bite after work, a family dinner or a long night with friends. Good food, fair prices and a warm welcome.
